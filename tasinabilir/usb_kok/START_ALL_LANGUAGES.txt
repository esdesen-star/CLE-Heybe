=== English (en) ===
- CLE Heybe — offline knowledge bag. It works without internet.
- To start: on a Mac double-click Heybe_Mac.command, on Windows double-click Heybe_Windows.bat.
- A page opens in the browser. Choose your language and wait until it says "Heybe is ready ✓" (1–3 minutes).
- Heybe answers only from the books on this USB drive and shows its sources. It is not a doctor: in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
