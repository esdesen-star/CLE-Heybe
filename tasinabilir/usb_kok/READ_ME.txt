WHAT HEYBE IS
Heybe is like a computer. Two things come ready on the stick: Heybe, which
answers questions, and a translator. The rest is yours: you fill the free
space with the books, notes and pictures you want.

STARTING
- Mac: double-click "Heybe_Mac.command".
  If the Mac blocks it the first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security →
  "Open Anyway" (older macOS: right-click the file → Open).
- Windows (10 or 11, 64-bit): double-click "Heybe_Windows.bat".
  If Windows warns you: "More info" → "Run anyway".
- Linux (x86_64, or 64-bit ARM such as Raspberry Pi): open a terminal in the drive's folder and type: sh Heybe_Linux.sh
  (Not tested on Linux yet — if you try it, please tell us how it went.)
- FIRST SETUP: if you downloaded Heybe from GitHub, the models are not in the
  package. The page tells you: "Library & settings" → "First setup" → accept
  the Gemma terms → "Download the models" (about 5 GB, internet needed once).
  If the download is cut, click again: it continues where it stopped.
- Heybe opens in your browser. First pick your language: all 49 languages
  are listed equally. A ✓ next to a language means the stick has books or
  files in it. Screen texts are machine translated in most languages, not
  yet tested. The library is ready at once. Heybe, which answers your
  questions, starts in 1–2 minutes depending on the stick. The translator
  starts only when needed (when "Also look in sources in other languages"
  is ticked) and joins a few minutes later.
- EMERGENCY CARD: shown in the first second. It has emergency numbers for
  several countries and your own card. Write your meeting place, your
  medicines, allergies and your family's phone numbers on it ("Write my
  emergency card"). The card stays on this stick and never goes online.
- To stop, press Enter / any key in the black window. Do not close the
  window with the X. Eject the stick in the computer before pulling it out.

ASK HEYBE
- Heybe answers only from the sources on this stick and shows them.
  If the sources don't say, it says it doesn't know.
- It looks first in books in your language. If they don't have the
  answer, it looks in books and files in other languages on the stick and
  translates the answer into your language. The source then says
  "translated", and you can open the original answer. Translation can make
  mistakes: for important information, check the source.
- It compares the numbers in its answer with the sources. If it sees a
  number that is not in the sources, or if the translation loses the topic
  of your question, it stops the answer and shows the sources instead.
- If an answer is wrong or incomplete, press "This answer is wrong or
  incomplete" below it and, if you like, write the right answer. The record
  stays only on this stick and is not sent anywhere; you can see it on the
  Library page, download it and give it to the people who develop Heybe.
  In the next version these questions are asked again.

LIBRARY — the free space is yours
- The "Library and settings" button on the start page. A guide is at the
  top of that page. If you pick a language with no books, Heybe suggests
  a ready list of free books for it; one click downloads it.
- "My book list": write the names of the books you want or add a ready
  list. If "automatically" is ticked, Heybe downloads missing books and
  fetches new versions whenever it is online (the old version is removed
  once the new one is verified).
- Each book has a health mark: can it be searched, can it be read.
- "Add from a link": a .zim book from another site, or a PDF, text or
  picture. A book from outside Kiwix is marked "not from Kiwix".
- In "Library" you can download books in any language when you have
  internet. One language per book is enough; Heybe translates when needed.
  Every downloaded book is checked to make sure it arrived complete.
- You can add your own PDFs, texts, pictures and videos — for example the
  assembly points in your area, your family's medicine list, your own notes.
  They stay on the stick and never go to the internet. Heybe answers from
  them too and marks them as "Your file".
- Give each picture and video a short description: Heybe finds them by it.
- It cannot yet read scanned PDFs (pages that are only pictures).
- If you copy a .zim book into the "kutuphane" (library) folder yourself,
  Heybe finds it too.
- The three small "Heybe test books" (Turkish, Czech, Chinese) are there to
  try the language bridge; you can remove them in "Library".
